How CampusLoop works

Trust checks come before your first message, not after. Buying should take two minutes or less.

  1. Sign in with your university email

    We confirm you are a student before you can list or message anyone.

  2. Browse or list

    Find textbooks, furniture and kitchenware near your campus, or list an item in a few taps.

  3. Check the trust signals

    The verified badge, pick-up point and seller history sit above the message button on every listing.

  4. Message and agree a time

    Keep the chat on the platform. No need to share your phone or address.

  5. Meet at a campus pick-up point

    Check the item, pay, and take it home. Bring a friend for large furniture.

What it costs

Basic listings are planned to be free. A small boosted-listing fee puts your item higher in search. There is no percentage fee on sales.

Common questions

Do I need to be a student?

Yes. Sign-in uses a university email address.

Can I get items posted to me?

No. Trading is face to face at campus pick-up points, which avoids lost or delayed parcels.

Is CampusLoop live?

Not yet. This is a prototype, and the waitlist helps us learn what students want first.

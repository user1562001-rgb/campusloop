Frequently asked questions

Is CampusLoop live?

Not yet. It is a prototype and the waitlist helps us learn what students want.

Who can use it?

Students with a university email address.

How do I get an item?

Meet at a campus pick-up point. There is no postage.

What does it cost?

Basic listings are planned to be free, with an optional boosted listing fee and no percentage fee.

How do you stop scams?

University email checks, seller history, and pick-ups at campus points. See the Trust Centre.

Can I sell furniture?

Yes, if you can bring it to a pick-up point or arrange pick-up with the buyer.

What if an item is not as described?

Do not pay. Walk away and report it.

How do I join?

Use the waitlist form on the contact page.

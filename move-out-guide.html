Move-out drives and guide

Semester-end drives

CampusLoop plans to run drives at the end of each semester where students list and hand over items in one place. Dates will be confirmed with each campus. See campus partners.

Selling before your lease ends keeps good items in use and puts cash back in your pocket.

Your move-out timeline

  1. Four weeks out: list big items

    Furniture takes longest to sell. Photograph in daylight.

  2. Three weeks out: sell textbooks

    Do it before exams end, when classmates still need them.

  3. Two weeks out: kitchenware and small items

    Bundle sets to sell faster.

  4. Last week: donate or recycle the rest

    Check your university or council for collection options.

Pricing tips

  • Check what similar items sell for, then price a little lower for a fast sale
  • Describe flaws honestly to avoid disputes
  • Offer pick-up windows to cut back-and-forth messages

Why it matters

Students often use items for one semester, then dispose of goods that still work. Reselling keeps them in circulation.

Trust Centre

How to avoid marketplace scams, and how CampusLoop is designed to reduce the risk.

Warning signs on any marketplace

  • Prices far below normal for the item
  • Seller asks you to pay before you see the item
  • Pressure to move the chat to another app
  • Photos that look copied, or descriptions that do not match
  • Seller refuses to meet in a public place

Safe pick-up checklist

  1. Meet on campus, in daylight

    Use a busy, designated pick-up point.

  2. Inspect before paying

    Check the item matches the listing. For textbooks, check the edition.

  3. Tell a friend

    Share the time and place.

  4. Report problems

    Flag misdescribed items or pressure tactics so the platform can act.

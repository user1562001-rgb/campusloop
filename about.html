About CampusLoop

A student marketplace idea built around one question: how can students trade useful things quickly, without worrying about scams?

The problem

Students move often and buy for a single semester. Good textbooks, furniture and kitchenware get thrown away. General marketplaces are cheap but risky, and platforms lose trust when fraud and delivery problems appear.

Our approach

  • University-email sign-in to prove identity
  • Designated campus pick-up points
  • A small boosted-listing fee instead of a percentage cut, to keep prices low
  • Semester-end move-out drives

Who it is for

Aarav, the buyer

23, international postgraduate sharing a Sydney flat. Wants to furnish a room cheaply within a week, with no car. Needs trusted sellers and pick-up near campus.

Mia, the seller

22, final-year undergraduate leaving Sydney in December. Wants to clear her room fast and recover some cash, with one-tap listing and no endless messages.

Aarav and Mia are draft personas to be tested through ten student interviews. AI-generated personas can carry bias (Salminen et al., 2024), so we treat them as starting points.

Honest status

CampusLoop is a university project prototype. There are no real users, sellers or listings yet. Sample listings are labelled as samples.

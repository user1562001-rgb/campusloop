Five ways to save money on study essentials

Small habits add up over a degree.

  1. Buy the previous edition

    Check with your lecturer if it covers the same content.

  2. Check the library first

    You may only need a book for a few weeks.

  3. Buy in bundles

    Sellers often discount sets of books or kitchenware.

  4. Pay at pick-up

    Inspect the item first, and meet at a campus point.

  5. Sell when you finish

    Resell textbooks and furniture to fund your next semester.

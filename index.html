Why students use CampusLoop

  • Verified members

Why trust is the whole product

A 2026 study of 1,000 second-hand market users in Korea found 19.2% had faced fraud or misdescribed goods and 21.5% had fulfilment problems such as missing parcels (Cho, 2026). That sample is not students and not Australian, so we are surveying Australian students to test the problem here.
